The three PDFs are two builds of one source, selected by the \ifanon toggle at
the top of asterism.tex (\anontrue = anonymous review; \anonfalse = named).
Asterism_SSRN_revised.pdf and Asterism_workshop_paper.pdf are the same named
build under two names, one for the SSRN posting and one as the camera-ready.

The 892 errors on the review build are all "Text bleeds into the {right,bottom}
margin", one per numbered line plus one per page footer. They are review-mode
furniture: acl.sty's [review] option prints line numbers and page numbers
outside the text block by design. The two builds differ only in the \ifanon
toggle, and the identical check on the named build is clean, which isolates
every finding to that furniture. aclpubcheck is a camera-ready instrument; it
is not meaningful against a [review] artifact. No overfull hboxes in either
build.

Page budget (NORA @ AACL 2026, long track, 8 pages of main body):
  main body ends p8 | Limitations + Ethics p9 | References p10 | Appendices p10-11
The limits bind the main body only; back matter and appendices are unlimited.

Anonymity (review build): no occurrence of the author name, handle,
affiliation phrase, live repository URL, or any address-like token.
Author line reads "Anonymous ACL submission"; the appendix cites the
anonymized code mirror. Embedded /Author metadata is empty.

Named build: author block and appendix both carry
https://anonymous.4open.science/r/asterism-7023, no anonymized-mirror reference, and
embedded /Author, /Title, /Subject, /Keywords metadata is populated for
public-repository indexing.
